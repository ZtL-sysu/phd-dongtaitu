# Offline experiment matrix preparation v1

This is a planning-only matrix prepared from the offline plan/RP snapshot, the execution contract, the root endpoint definitions, P4 pilot v3 and existing queue/config manifests. It is not an accepted launch queue, test authorization, scientific freeze, or result. Every future checkpoint/config/output hash is null; no candidate identifier, endpoint tolerance, operating point or outcome is selected. Existing P4 `g00/m00` refer only to that accepted bounded pilot queue and are not automatically carried into this formal matrix.

The machine-readable inventory is `protocols/offline-required-experiment-matrix-preparation-v1.json`. It expands the primary core training requirements to 90 planning rows: V0/V1/V2/V3/V4/V9 × synthetic/email/MOOC × model seeds 0–4. V5/V6/V7 replay the same-seed V4 checkpoint, and V8 replays same-seed V2. It separately lists training/independently fitted contrasts and frozen replay controls: shuffled-c, constant-g, unconditional neighbor reset, frozen tau, change-only/hybrid/periodic-level encoding, and the continuous-state comparator.

The synthetic list has nine structural/scale cases: identity 10/30%, fixed-support weight 10/30%, gradual weight-128 fixed support, nonlinear tanh, noise-only, local shock, and N256 identity-30 scale. Missingness is kept separate: 4 rates × 3 corruption mechanisms × 4 dataset regimes (48 design cells), with identical whole-trajectory masks across methods and clean targets. The scheduling section enumerates all 16 graph/state quota pairs and ages 2/4/8, while preserving the distinction between the validation-selected H3 operating point and required secondary grid; devices are CPU/CUDA. The profile section records B1/FP32, 10 warmups, 5 timed full-origin replays. Episode fitting/splits and train-only activity/case-slice requirements are separately explicit. H1/H2/H3 contain 9/16/24 primary family members and require paired temporal-block statistics with Holm within each family.

Two chronology clarifications supersede wording in the earlier planning gap audit without changing it: P3A freezes prospective rules, allowed inputs and fixed output paths; it cannot hash or bind not-yet-trained P4 checkpoints. After P4 selection, a pretest execution binding records the actual checkpoint/freeze/policy/block/epsilon before test access, and postrun manifests hash outputs. Also, `p3-gru-replication-queue-v1.json` is `prepared-not-launched` and requires successful immutable P3 serial continuation plus source-bound seed0 queues; it is not yet an unblocked runner.

SeqSNN’s seed rule is conditional rather than fully resolved: the RP requires five seeds for principal comparisons, the replacement decision keeps SeqSNN in the compatible forecasting campaign, and current B4 traceability says formal/final comparison is pending. Existing SeqSNN configs are seed0. The records do not say unambiguously whether SeqSNN is a principal five-seed comparator; root must decide that scope before P3A. If principal, use seeds 0–4; otherwise retain only the seed0 development comparator. The accepted email-episode supplement requires its own train fit/checkpoint/validation choice and is additive, not a replacement for original email or part of the 24 main H3 endpoints; its blanket formal seed count also remains unspecified.
