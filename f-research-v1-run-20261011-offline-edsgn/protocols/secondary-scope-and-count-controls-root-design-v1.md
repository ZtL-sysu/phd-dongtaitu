# Secondary experiment allocation and count controls

Root protocol decision during unfinished P3, before proposed-model training,
final test access, or any oracle analysis. This design fixes the following
scientific choices; it does not pass P3A, admit resources, select g/m or a
scheduler, or constitute an executed experiment. The complete concrete matrix
and source/config bindings still have to pass review before P3A.

The original synthetic, original email, and MOOC V0/V1/V2/V3/V4/V9 comparisons
retain model seeds 0–4. The principal GRU/DGCRN/SeqSNN comparisons and the
registered 49-row H1/H2/H3 family also retain their five seeds. No broad
secondary diagnostic replaces a principal replication.

The additive email-episode model study uses seed0 for V0/V1/V2/V3/V4/V9 and
the V4-derived V5/V6/V7 and V2-derived V8 policies. The already accepted GRU
seeds0–4 queue is preserved, as are the actual source-bound seed0 DGCRN,
SeqSNN and compatible external baseline comparisons. These core/policy
episode results are explicitly one-seed descriptive diagnostics, fitted and
calibrated only on that episode's own training and validation partitions.
They do not enter the original-email primary H3 family or identify its
noninferiority margin.

The independent graph/state quota sensitivity grid uses model seed0, all16
pairs of fractions0.25/0.5/0.75/1, and maximum ages2/4/8 on both original
email and MOOC. Adaptive SNN, adaptive ANN, periodic and random policies use
the same frozen checkpoint appropriate to each method, common full origins,
and their declared validation-only calibration rules. The full reference can
be reused only when checkpoint, input, policy, device and replay contracts
are exactly identical. Complete CPU/CUDA B1/FP32 timings retain10 warmups and
five repeats. This grid is descriptive sensitivity evidence; its test results
cannot select the primary policy or support five-seed inference. Full grid
resource admission is pending and no row is waived by this decision.

N256 uses its own independently trained V2 and V4 seed0 models and its own
training-derived normalization, residual scale, candidate buffers and runtime
geometry. It is a scale/engineering diagnostic with full versus selective
frozen replay, not a new principal hypothesis family. N64 weights will not
be relabeled N256. The exact N256 configs, validation-only policy calibration,
actual representative training/replay resource measurement and matrix rows
must be bound before execution. The accepted N64 principal studies remain
unchanged.

The twelve existing missingness recipes remain separate from structural
scenario checks. Count-noise and event-time perturbations are separate clean
missingness-rate0 recipes: integer uniform input-count noise in[-1,+1],
clipped at zero, and event-prior time jitter of at most one bin, respectively.
Both use recipe seed2027 and the same perturbation for every compared method.
No count noise is applied to signed synthetic values. Future events are
filtered using their true time before jitter, and clean targets/masks are
preserved. Combining these perturbations with every missingness/scenario
setting is not part of the initial matrix. The exact compared method/seed
and recipe rows still require complete prospective admission; this paragraph
does not claim their execution or settle their resource cost.

RP section5.4 also requires comparing a count-appropriate training loss with
squared error. The prior required-coverage extraction omitted this item;
P3A completeness must therefore explicitly include it. The controlled
secondary comparison is V4 with its mandatory nonnegative count head and
masked Poisson negative log likelihood versus the same selected V4 trained
with the existing masked squared error, on original email and MOOC seed0.
Use raw nonnegative count targets; compute the Poisson loss with log_input
false, full false, reduction none, a positive prediction floor1e-8, and
normalization by valid target cells. Preserve graph regularization, optimizer,
epochs/patience, architecture, observation access, task and total state width.
This comparison is descriptive, cannot choose the primary g/m configuration,
and adds no loss to signed synthetic training. Its adapter/controller,
provenance binding, CPU fixtures, concrete configs and actual full
train/validation numeric acceptance are still pending. Do not modify an
active or completed source-bound training implementation to add this control.

All allocations remain within the existing single cumulative72-hour campaign
and baseline-first phase gates. Current measured evidence does not establish
that the complete matrix fits the remaining budget. If it does not fit, keep
unrun rows and the goal incomplete; do not reduce required windows, omit rows,
reset accounting, or report preparation as completion.
