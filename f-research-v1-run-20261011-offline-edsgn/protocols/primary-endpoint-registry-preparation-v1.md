# Primary endpoint registry preparation v1

Status: **preparation draft only**. This is a requirements extraction and endpoint-schema proposal, not an accepted P3A plan, a statistical run, or evidence about any hypothesis. I read the execution contract, implementation plan, claim-evidence matrix, family-assembly requirement, current paired-statistics/Holm staging, and the frozen graph/H2 diagnostic producers. No arrays, checkpoints, models, or outcomes were loaded; no statistics were run.

The binding registry needs three complete, non-shrinkable primary families. Before P4 test access, each family must expand into every preregistered domain × condition × contrast × outcome row with an immutable producer, direction, legal temporal slice, required horizons where relevant, five model seeds, frozen block rule, and output path. Apply Holm once over the entire H1, H2, or H3 p-value set after all required strata exist. A missing or unidentifiable row blocks the family; it is not dropped. Use seeds 0–4, 2,000 paired temporal block draws with seed 1234, and a shared legal origin-block schedule within each aligned domain. Never let a temporal block cross a trajectory boundary. Report model-seed spread separately from temporal uncertainty.

Forecast endpoints use raw valid-cell pooled MAE per required horizon, equal weighting over the predeclared horizons, and equal weighting over five seeds. Per-horizon MAE/RMSE, activity strata, and extra slices remain secondary unless registered as primary before test. For email the required horizons are 1/3/7; for MOOC they are 1/6/24. Synthetic horizon selection must be copied from its approved task contract. The block length is explicitly pending baseline calibration; it must follow the accepted P3A rule, fit the shortest declared trajectory support, and never be capped to manufacture coverage. Zero support is unidentifiable.

The candidate minimum mechanism coverage is:

- **H1:** noise-only false graph variation (V4/V9) plus response under declared structural changes and forecast preservation within a validation-calibrated tolerance. Keep V0/V1 and periodic-refresh comparisons as preregistered context/control evidence. Retain all declared identity/weight-change, 10%/30%, and missingness conditions; the root must freeze the exact expanded strata.
- **H2:** post-change error/recovery and informative-c specificity against shuffled-c, constant-g, and unconditional neighbor-reset controls at matched width. Include V3/V4 and V1/V2 context. Require the prescribed-graph matched-retention supplement: its per-step teacher graph/action proof prevents later graph differences from being mistaken for memory-specific effects. In ordinary arms, retention changes can alter predictions, residuals, and later adaptive graphs; the v4 diagnostic explicitly disclaims whole-window graph equality.
- **H3:** V7 compared with full V4, periodic V5, and random V6 on accuracy tolerance and paired measured cost; V7/V8 is the separate SNN-value comparison. Costs require full replay, synchronized whole-window timing, CPU and CUDA separately, and action/age/overrun plus memory counters. A reduction in actions alone does not establish acceleration.

The JSON stores these endpoint templates and producer output requirements, while marking exact slice choices and calibration as unresolved. It does not invent epsilon or block-length numbers. One implementation blocker is already visible: `claim_family_holm_v2.py` currently accepts only the forecast required-horizon MAE outcome type. Non-MAE graph-TV, recovery/noninferiority, and cost endpoints need a versioned producer and raw-p schema before they can participate in registered family inference.

Open decisions for root/P3A: exact synthetic primary strata/horizons; post-change slice and recovery functional; inclusion of real-data strata in H1/H2 mechanism families; validation-derived epsilon and legal block lengths; and a sound uncertainty producer for repeated timing measurements. Full detail is in the companion JSON.
