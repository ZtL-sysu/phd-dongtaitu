Frozen during unfinished P3, before any test prediction or metric. The original
calendar, training cohort, training statistics, checkpoints and required test
coverage remain unchanged. Source audit found zero released email records in
the entire original validation partition; both observed count features and all
valid validation targets are zero. This is a source-coverage limitation.

budgets on validation and may be reported descriptively. All-zero valid targets
make accuracy-based selection and a substantive epsilon calibration unavailable.
The program must record `primary_quality_identifiable=false`,
`epsilon_constraint_satisfied=false`, and
`selection_status=unidentifiable-all-zero-validation-targets`.

For technical frozen replay, use the already preregistered s00 policy (graph and
state fractions .5, age caps4, median causal monitor-score thresholds), regardless
of candidate error or runtime ordering. Its thresholds may summarize only the
allowed original validation histories; they are not evidence of informative
quality discrimination. Attach that device's measured B1 costs before its
validation replay and retain the exact executed policy. Use a predeclared block
length equal to the history length28, rather than claiming an identifiable error
ACF. A caller-supplied .02 tolerance is an externally fixed descriptive slice;
also report 0/1/2/5 percent slices without selecting a new policy on test.

This regime cannot support a positive primary H3 accuracy-constrained selection
claim from that validation set. Test prediction, actual timing, counters and
descriptive error-cost slices are still required and will disclose the limitation.
Do not import episode validation decisions, statistics, weights or epsilon:
those days are included in the original-calendar training partition. The earliest
episode has a separate fitting/calibration protocol and offers a same-source
temporal-regime diagnostic, never independent external confirmation.

For a nondegenerate validation set, retain ordinary validation-only selection.
If no candidate meets the frozen tolerance, record no feasible selective policy
and fall back explicitly to the full reference; do not present the minimum-MAE
candidate as satisfying the failed constraint.
