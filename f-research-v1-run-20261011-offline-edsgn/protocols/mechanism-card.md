# Mechanism card — planned evidence

Problem: forecasting on a fixed training-defined entity set with changing predictive dependencies, corrupted/missing inputs, and avoidable refresh computation.

Mechanism1: a shared quality-aware candidate edge scorer, bounded contact/exploration pool and top-k support; reliability-adjusted temporal penalty relaxes for credible residual changes. Falsifier: constant-confidence V9 changes less under noise and tracks shifts at least as well at matched candidate/width/information.

Mechanism2: fixed total-width fast/slow local/neighbor state. Graph row TV c is computed after authorized refresh, and only old neighbor state receives accumulated exp(−βc) at its next expensive update. LIF delayed reset and surrogate gradients are real computations. Falsifier: V3/uncoupled, shuffled-c or generic constant forgetting matches recovery, or capacity/history explains gains.

Mechanism3: causal cheap monitor, separate graph/state indices, maximum ages and mandatory audit; execute only authorized expensive rows/nodes and report source union/base readout overhead. Falsifier: periodic/random or continuous-state gates dominate end-to-end accuracy–time, or indexing/monitor overhead removes savings.

Contribution boundaries: learned graph edges are predictive associations; only restricted synthetic diagnostics have known generating edges. Hybrid continuous encoder/LIF/readout software does not imply neuromorphic energy savings. Independent-window cost does not establish streaming deployment efficiency. No prior-art originality claim is accepted before literature and matched evidence.
