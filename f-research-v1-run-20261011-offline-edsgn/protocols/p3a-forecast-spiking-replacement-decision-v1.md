# Prospective executable spiking forecasting comparator decision

Root applies the accepted bounded rule in `protocols/p1b-protocol-repairs-v1.md`
before any final test access. The October11 public-source pass is preserved in
`reviews/p3a-spiking-comparator-disposition-v3.md` and its raw status log, together
with the earlier code/fidelity screen. SpikeSTAG is a relevant forecasting
method, but author code was not verified and its existing local exploratory
implementation is not faithful. SpikySpace is also forecasting, and its linked
public anonymous archive exposed no inspectable source tree, pinned version or
license to the available reader. These findings document current accessibility,
not nonexistence or poor measured performance. No authentication workaround or
paper score is used.

The evidence-backed executable replacement is the author-linked Microsoft
SeqSNN iSpikformer, pinned at15cbacb123362c97c0aa0f6f861c18f4f77e3e8f, MIT.
It retains the official genuine spiking forecasting backbone and declared
encoder, attention, internal steps and hidden dimensions. Its historical input
and raw output head follow the same node cohort, causal partitions, L/H/F and
required scoring offsets as the other task adaptations. CPU tests and actual
full-size train-only CUDA probes established its runnable source/dependency
route; full train/validation is now executing under at-launch bindings. Source,
task adaptations and repository/paper encoder discrepancy are disclosed in
`reviews/external-baseline-code-audit.md`. It is labeled SeqSNN-adapted and is
never labeled SpikeSTAG/SpikySpace or a reproduction of their methods.

Selection is based on verified source, forecasting compatibility and genuine
SNN execution, before observing this campaign's validation scores. It is not
selected because another comparator scored poorly. This decision satisfies the
accepted alternative-route rule for an executable recent spiking forecaster;
its actual numeric/reproducibility evidence must still pass P3/P3A.

All directly compatible RP forecasting methods remain in the campaign:
DGCRN, Graph WaveNet, AGCRN, STID, STAEformer and DCRNN. The primary scientific
decisions remain the registered confidence, relation-retention and frozen
independent G/S interventions, with matched ANN/SNN controls and uncertainty.
The project makes no broad superiority claim over unavailable SpikeSTAG or
SpikySpace, and no first-graph-SNN claim. These papers remain relevant novelty
context with explicit acquisition/fidelity limitations. Classification-only
SpikeNet/Delay-DSGN/ChronoSpike retain their documented task boundaries.

This is a prospective baseline-route decision under the unchanged v1 contract,
not a passed P3/P3A, an architecture replacement, or a hypothesis result.
