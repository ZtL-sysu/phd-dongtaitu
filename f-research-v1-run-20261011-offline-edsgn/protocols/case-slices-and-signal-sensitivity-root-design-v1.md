# Prospective report slices and within-model signal sensitivity

This fixes unfinished required case-report rules without observing final
predictions, targets or oracle annotations. It supplements the accepted
training activity strata. It is not an executed case study, a new primary
hypothesis, a gate pass or an inference authorization.

For every domain, retain the complete ordered legal origins and divide each
trajectory into consecutive nonoverlapping blocks of L origins, starting at
its first legal origin. Keep the final short block explicitly. These are the
predefined report slices; no advantageous alternative boundaries are chosen
after outcomes. Synthetic/reporting-only switch-relative plots retain their
separate previously fixed4L rule after full global closure.

Input-change scores use permitted observed history only: average the absolute
difference of adjacent raw observations divided by the frozen training robust
scale, pooling valid adjacent time/node/feature pairs inside each origin's
L-bin history. Missing adjacent pairs are excluded and zero-support scores
remain null. Freeze the score definition and the linear25th/75th percentiles
fitted solely on that domain's training histories before test. Stable input
slices have pooled valid-pair score no greater than the training25th percentile;
high-change slices have score at least the training75th percentile. Choose the
first complete L-origin qualifying slice in ordered trajectory/origin order,
not the slice with a favorable method difference. If no slice qualifies,
report unavailable. This score labels observable input variation; it does not
assert that a real-data relationship switched.

The worst case is the predefined slice with the largest V4 seed0 raw MAE,
pooling valid cells per required forecast offset and then equally averaging
offsets. Ties select the earliest trajectory/block. It is explicitly a
post-completion descriptive worst slice, never a primary endpoint or policy
selection input. All slices and their support/errors remain reported so the
selected display cannot hide weak or unsupported regions. Plot V4 alongside
its V3 uncoupled and V9 confidence-off interventions, compact GRU and DGCRN,
using each regime's own fitted/frozen checkpoint. No favorable comparator is
chosen on final outcomes. Episode figures retain their independent source/split.

Long inactive gaps are an additional node/origin grouping: every cell of a
node's provided L-bin input history is observed and all raw count inputs are
zero. This applies only to the three count regimes; missing cells and signed
synthetic observations do not count as inactivity. Preserve a separate
incomplete-history group. The rule uses allowed history, while forecasting
metrics use only clean valid targets. Report counts and pooled-cell/equal-
offset errors without replacing frozen training-activity strata or primary
families.

The required highlighted-signal sensitivity must have a separately reviewed
frozen driver before P3A. Choose a focal node by largest training activity
score (synthetic mean absolute magnitude), breaking ties by frozen node ID.
Choose its highlighted neighbor from the largest mean valid neighbor weight
in that node's V4 seed0 full validation replay, breaking ties by neighbor ID;
if there is no valid nonself neighbor, keep an unavailable diagnostic rather
than invent an edge. Selection is validation-only, independent of final errors
and structural oracle. Bind node/neighbor IDs, validation full-origin record,
source/checkpoint hashes and selection rule before test.

The relationship-removal arm reruns the same frozen V4 while excluding that
one directed neighbor from the focal node's eligible graph candidate/support
at every graph refresh. It preserves the original candidate cap and local
path, renormalizes the remaining valid support, handles empty support explicitly
and recomputes actual relation change on the altered support. It does not
silently enlarge the candidate set or fit weights. The retention-removal arm
uses the original V4 teacher graph/action replay and sets the focal node's
neighbor retention factor to1 at the same consumption times; local channels,
deferred reset and all other nodes remain unchanged. Informative teacher parity,
identical graph/actions in the retention arm and unchanged parameter bytes are
required engineering checks. These arms are sensitivities of an identified
signal, not causal discovery or independently trained principal contrasts.

Both arms cover every legal test origin of N64-main, original email, MOOC and
the episode at model seed0, yielding eight additional logical arm rows. They
must be part of the prospective complete frozen test inventory, complete before
any oracle/reporting join, and be included in resource/budget accounting.
Displayed stable/high/worst slices are selected only after this complete
execution, under the frozen rules above. No future selection, checkpoint,
freeze, driver or completion hash is populated by this design. Actual training-
only threshold fit, validation neighbor selection, causal driver, complete
admission/closure and all report metrics remain unfinished.
